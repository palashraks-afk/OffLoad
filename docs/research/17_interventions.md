# 17. What has been tried (read 2026-10-25)

| Intervention | What I read | Evidence of effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Late Shift Connect, Broward County (Florida)** | NACo 2023 award page (https://www.naco.org/resources/award-programs/broward-county-transit-late-shift-connect-program): eligible late-shift workers who live and work in the county get a payment card with five subsidized trips a week, up to $15 a trip after a $2 co-pay, for rides between 11 p.m. and 6 a.m. when fixed-route buses do not run. The county later raised a taxi limit to 10 trips a week (via search summary). | The page reports no ridership or outcome numbers; a county survey was running (results not found). Treat as a promising design, not a proven one. |
| **Transit subsidy for job seekers** | Phillips RCT in DC (doc 12). | Job finding within 40 days +9 points; faded by 90 days (not significant). |
| **Vanpool subsidy, SBCTA SB Loop** | $600 a month per vanpool (up from $400), up to $700 zero-emission, announced 7 August 2023 (https://www.gosbcta.com/vanpool-subsidies-increase-in-the-inland-empire/). | No evaluation found. |
| **First/last-mile employer shuttle (Omnitrans Route 300)** | Omnitrans FY2026 plan: fewer than three passengers per weekday on 25 trips; proposed for elimination after three years. | Evidence that a shuttle for downtown San Bernardino workers did not attract riders, despite marketing. Not evidence about shuttles in general. |
| **Late-night network, King County Metro Night Owl** | Via search summary: 2017 expansion of about 11,000 annual hours; more than 4,500 survey responses where late- and early-shift workers asked for better options; a 2022 University of Washington thesis (Margetic) found late-night trips take significantly longer and large areas lack meaningful late-night access. | Descriptive; not a causal evaluation. |
| **Employer commute services (for example "Shift" in South Metro Atlanta)** | Employer page read: surveys, commute dashboards, vanpool and shuttle setup. | No outcome data on the page. |

## What the app can honestly say
- A 2023 vanpool subsidy exists for San Bernardino County (up to $600 a month per vanpool).
- Late-shift ride subsidies exist elsewhere (Broward). No evaluation of their effect was found.
- Do not claim that any of these "works".
