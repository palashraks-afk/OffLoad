# What riders and workers worry about most (Reddit titles and snippets, 2026-10-25)

Reddit pages could not be opened; these are titles and first lines from search results, mostly r/InlandEmpire. They show the topics, not how common each is. Counter-evidence: Omnitrans' own survey reports 83% overall satisfaction two years running (omnitrans.org).

| Concern | What the snippets say | What the app does about it | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buses late, early, or skipping | "late, super early, or don't even show up"; "the bus passed me twice today"; "bus runs get missed due to call outs" (driver shortage) | Live-alert and tracking links; be-at-stop-early tip; plan B (next bus if you miss it) | No live positions (Omnitrans' alert API needs a private token) |
| Strikes and holidays | A labor-action post (April 2025); no service in the High Desert and Riverside on Juneteenth | Link to live alerts | The timetable has no holiday calendar |
| No night or weekend service | "no service at night or on weekends" on some routes; only four 15-minute routes in San Bernardino | The core of the app: 11 PM coverage, last bus home, hour strip | |
| Waiting at stops: no shelter, no stop nearby, safety at night | "A bus stop isn't shelter"; "my neighborhood doesn't even have a bus stop"; fear of waiting after 10 pm | Stop-level night-time death counts near the stops used, conditions card (temperature, UV, air quality) at the stop | No shade or lighting data for stops |
| Cost of the alternative | Uber "eats up half of my paycheck" | Cost card, vanpool subsidy, cost per resident | |
| Jobs scheduled without regard to transit | "scheduling shifts at hours when public transportation doesn't run" (r/AmazonFC) | Employer view of reachable start times; message to boss | |
| Long and tiring work days | 12-hour warehouse days; commutes of 45-90 minutes (San Bernardino County report) | Door-to-door time; health card | |
| Getting started | "How do I start riding the bus? Which apps do I need?" | Plain-language results, Spanish | |

Two ideas this table suggested and the app now has or should have: a **plan B** when the bus does not come, and **specific situations** in About showing how real trips turn out.
