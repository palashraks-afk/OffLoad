## Patterns
1. A true personal reason behind the app (about 8 of 10).
2. A visible AI or forecasting component.
3. One specific user (farmers, small water-system operators, clinicians), not "everyone".
4. A problem statable in one sentence (the video rules require it).
5. **None of the winner pages show measured impact or user testing**, including the overall winner. Real evidence is therefore a way to stand out, not a requirement.
6. Crowded lanes: wildfire (3+), water/irrigation (3), AI chest X-ray (4), climate education. Less crowded: transportation/road safety, public-health data for local governments, language access.
7. Scale: about 4,600 apps across 394 districts (about 12 per district). The goal is to be best in CA-33.
8. Past CA-33 winners were both environment/health + AI, both involving Rancho Cucamonga High School (local competition).
