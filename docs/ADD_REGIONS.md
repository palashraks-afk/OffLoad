## The honest limit
The app supports **one bus agency per region**. Most of CA-22 and CA-23 is served by several small agencies, so "all the cities in the district" cannot be done correctly without merging feeds (a planner change). A region that quietly leaves out some cities' buses would show a misleadingly low "share near a bus". Use "the part of the district served by agency X" and say so on screen.
Also: the app already has 3 regions (CA-33, Cleveland, Miami). More regions help "Nationwide" only a little. Finish the health/cost additions, record the video and submit first (deadline Mon 2026-10-26 9:00 am Pacific).

## How to add one (the student does it; about 1 hour each if nothing breaks)
From `shiftready/README.md`: copy a region file in `shiftready/regions/` (Cleveland's `cle.json` is the template for a non-California region: it omits the California-only `facilities` key), change the id, names, GTFS URL, state/county FIPS, `boundary` (state and district), `lodes_state`, FARS codes, UTM zone and map center; then run `fetch_region.py <id>`, `osm.py <id>`, `run_all.py`.
Safe process (important a day before submission):
1. `git tag safe-before-regions` and work on a branch. The deployed site must keep working.
2. After each region, run all tests (`node shiftready/tests/*.test.js` and `pytest shiftready/tests`).
3. Check the numbers by eye (share near a bus at noon and 11 pm should be plausible; region name correct in English and Spanish).
4. Merge only if everything passes. If a region fails, drop it. Do not spend more than 3 hours total.
Pitfalls seen so far: Census Reporter blocks generic user agents; OSM's public Overpass server rate-limits (HTTP 429), so `osmnx` can fail on big areas; the fetch script's user agent contains an email address and this repository is public (consider removing it).

Sacramento first (verified feed, strong story: "the state capital's own bus service at 11 pm"), then CA-23 valley only if entering CA-23, then DC if the key is easy. Skip CA-22 and the High Desert.

## Update 2026-10-25: Sacramento added

The road-risk model was not run for it (needs OSM data and `osmnx`), so the risk layers and corridors are absent there (the app shows them only when present). ## Update 2026-10-25: CA-23 added and made the default (the entry district)
Region `ca23` (California's 23rd District; the student's school, Redlands High School, is in CA-23). Built with the same pipeline and `gtfs_extra` merging.

**In (all verified to download and build):** Omnitrans (main agency; valley, Redlands, Yucaipa, Loma Linda, Highland), Metrolink commuter rail (includes the Redlands stops), Victor Valley Transit (Victorville, Hesperia, Apple Valley, Adelanto, Barstow; feed `api.transloc.com/gtfs/vvta.zip`), Morongo Basin Transit (Yucca Valley, Twentynine Palms), Mountain Transit (Big Bear). 3,705 stops, 85 routes, 5,713 trips. Random-trip planner check: 154 trips, 0 failures.

**Honest limits**
- The app covers the **San Bernardino County part** of CA-23 only (the county the Census, LODES, ACS and crash files were pulled for). CA-23's northern part (Inyo County) is not included. Population in the district inside the county: 729,052; jobs 172,039.
- **Victor Valley Transit's published timetable only runs 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-28.** The app shows the earliest end date over all feeds ("Timetable valid through 2026-11-28") and the weekly check should be re-run after that date. After 28 Nov 2026 the desert part will be out of date until a new feed is built.
- Coverage numbers ("near a bus") count **buses only**: Metrolink is merged for trip planning but excluded from coverage (`"mode": "rail"` in the region file). Before 2026-10-25 the people step ignored extra feeds entirely; it now includes extra bus feeds. CA-33's numbers did not change because it only has Metrolink as an extra feed.
- Fares: only Omnitrans fares are in the app. Legs on other operators are not priced; the fare card says "Omnitrans fare only (other operators extra)" and hides when no leg is Omnitrans. The monthly pass comparison is hidden when any leg is another operator. The what-if route extension lists Omnitrans routes only (the $141/hour cost figure is Omnitrans' national transit data, 2022).
- No road-risk model layers for ca23 (needs OSM data and `osmnx`); the app hides those layers when absent. Past-death counts near stops (FARS) are included.
- Injury statistics for CA-23 were recomputed with the same method as CA-33 (FARS 2019-2023, polygon of CA-23 inside the county): 201 people killed walking or biking, 84% in the dark, 62% died at the scene, 81% within an hour, 89% on roads posted 40 mph or higher, 6,271 years of life lost before 75. Script: `analysis/health_stats_ca23.py`; data: `data/research/ca23_health_deaths_2019_2023.csv`.
- Employers: the EDD major-employer list is county-wide; only 4 of its employers lie inside CA-23 (Esri, Loma Linda University Health, Redlands Community Hospital, Yaamava' Resort & Casino). Quick-pick also lists hospitals, clinics and dialysis centres.
- Not merged: Riverside Transit, Foothill Transit, Barstow Area Transit (now part of VVTA), Beaumont, Needles, and any agency in Inyo or Mono County.
- Live alerts: the card links Omnitrans alerts and the websites of the other operators (no live feeds).

Correction to the CA-33 note above: it said coverage was unchanged "because the new stops sit where buses already reach". The real reason is that the coverage step did not read the extra feed at all, and rail is now deliberately excluded.

## Update 2026-10-25: Denver, Atlanta, Seattle, Boston added
Four more regions built with the same pipeline from each agency's public GTFS file (no key): RTD (CO-1, Denver County), MARTA (GA-5, Fulton County), King County Metro (WA-7, King County), MBTA (MA-8, Suffolk County). Each is the county plus the congressional district containing the city centre (Census 119th-Congress file); the share, population and death counts are for that district; the county bounds the tract list. Philadelphia (SEPTA's file is a zip of zips), Chicago (CTA, 69 MB) and Houston (no direct feed link found) were not added.
- **Service selection:** these feeds list many overlapping or one-day service periods, so `prep/transit.py` has a new option (`typical_service: true` in the region file) that picks, for weekdays, Saturdays and Sundays, the most common service pattern over the next 6 weeks (reference dates 2026-10-25 and later). Regions without that flag are built exactly as before (checked: ca23 output identical). Cleveland, Miami and Sacramento were not rebuilt with it.
- **FARS:** the national 2019-2023 pedestrian/cyclist file was built with `analysis/load_fars.py` (the old local file was only California). The California file did not change, and re-running safety for ca23 and ca33 left their data identical.
- **Not for these regions:** road-risk model and layers, hospital/clinic/dialysis list (California-only list), employers list, fares (the fare reader only knows Omnitrans' layout; the cost card is hidden), the statistics card and the injury and cost-per-hour lines. Region picker and About page hide California-only sources for them.
- **Headline, share of people within a 5-minute walk of a bus at 11 PM (weekday):** Denver 54%, Atlanta 77%, Seattle 72%, Boston 82% (Boston's district part is small: about 179,000 people). The Atlanta noon and 11 PM figures are equal because nearly every MARTA stop has a departure in both hours.
- **Known edge (all regions):** if origin and destination are within about 800 m the planner reports no bus; the page says to walk (one random Atlanta case hit it).
- **To rebuild:** `python shiftready/prep/run_all.py den atl sea bos`.

## Update 2026-10-25: road-risk model for CA-23
`prep/risk_model.py ca23` now runs. Road data: OpenStreetMap, six boxes around the populated parts of the San Bernardino County part of CA-23 (Redlands to Yucaipa in two boxes, Big Bear, Victorville to Apple Valley, Barstow, Yucca Valley to Twentynine Palms), listed as `osm_tiles` in `regions/ca23.json`; 34,696 nodes and 92,042 segments. The desert in between has no roads in the model, so only 246 of the 535 county deaths in the model box snapped to a surveyed road within 60 m; the others lie outside the boxes (including the CA-33 side of San Bernardino). The main Overpass server (overpass-api.de) refused this computer (TLS failure with curl, HTTP 406 from the browser), so the data came from the mirror overpass.openstreetmap.fr (set `OVERPASS_URL`). The mirror returned 4 ways whose nodes were missing; the merge step now drops such ways (4 dropped).
Validation design unchanged (train 2019-2021, test 2022-2023, 122 test deaths, 91,362 segments). Result, as reported by the model: at the top 20% of road length the road-features model captured 87% of later deaths against 68% for the best past-deaths baseline (+21 points, 95% interval +12 to +30); at the top 10% it was 66% against 64% (+3, interval -5 to +11, a tie); model plus past deaths on the same road was best at the top 10% (69%, +6, interval +2 to +10). Small test set, so these are estimates.
