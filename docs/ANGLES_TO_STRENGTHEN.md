## The chain that connects all four gaps (this is the story for the video)
1. **Work:** people who work shifts and do not drive cannot get to or from work when the bus stops. In CA-33, 37% of residents live near a bus at noon, 4% at 11 pm, 0% at 1 am (app's own numbers; Omnitrans only).
2. **Sleep and health:** night-shift workers are far more likely to be short on sleep. CDC (2010, National Health Interview Survey): short sleep (6 hours or less) was 44.0% for usual night-shift workers vs 28.8% for day-shift; in **transportation and warehousing night shift 69.7%**, in health care 52.3%. Source: https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/mm6116a2.htm
3. **Driving tired is the fallback when there is no bus:** in a small track study of 16 night-shift workers (PNAS 2015), 37.5% had a near-crash on the drive home after a night shift and none after a normal night's sleep (small sample; indicative, not population-level). A National Sleep Foundation position statement cites a 2008 poll where shift workers were about 3 times as likely as day workers to drive drowsy. https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2015/12/151221193522.htm and https://www.sleephealthjournal.org/article/S2352-7218(25)00225-6/fulltext
4. **Walking at night is the other fallback, and that is where people die:** see the CA-33 injury profile below.
5. **Fix:** ask for the service, change the shift start, or organize a subsidized vanpool, with real costs next to each.
Do not say the app "prevents" crashes. Say the link between short sleep, drowsy driving and night walking is documented, and the app shows people their options and gives agencies a costed request.

### Workplace heat panel for warehouse workers (information, not legal advice)
- Cal/OSHA indoor heat illness prevention standard, Title 8 section 3396, effective 2024-07-23: baseline duties (water, cool-down area below 82 F, rest, written plan, training) where indoor temperature is 82 F or higher; additional measures at 87 F (temperature or heat index) where feasible; employers must measure and record. Sources: https://perkinscoie.com/insights/update/calosha-publishes-indoor-heat-illness-prevention-standard and https://dir.ca.gov/DOSH/HeatIllnessInfo.html (verify the current text).

### What can and cannot be said about heat and health

San Bernardino County had among the largest case counts 2015-2024 (a count, not a rate).
- A 2024 report quoted the San Bernardino County health department saying it lacked reliable county data on heat-related deaths and ED visits. Do NOT quote a county heat-illness rate.
