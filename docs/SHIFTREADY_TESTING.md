# 3-person test (for the Works score)

Goal: three people who did not help build it use the live link. Do not coach them. Record what happens honestly, including failures.

## Pick testers
- One shift worker or commuter without a car (or a family member of one).
- One person who reads mostly Spanish (use the Spanish toggle). 
- One older adult or teen. Anyone who did not see the app before.

## Give each person three tasks
1. "Find out if you could get from [their home] to [a place they go] by 8 AM on a weekday."
2. "Find out when the last bus home is if you finish work at 10 PM."
3. "Write a request to the bus company."

## Watch and note
Time to finish each task, where they paused or tapped the wrong thing, words they did not understand, whether they trusted the answer, whether they would use it again.
