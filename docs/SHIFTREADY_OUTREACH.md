## Email draft (edit in your own words, send from your own email)
Subject: Student app: bus coverage at night and on weekends in our area

Hello, I am a student in [your city] and built a free web app, Shift Ready, for the Congressional App Challenge. Using Omnitrans' published schedule and Census data, it shows that in CA-33 only about 4% of residents live within a 5-minute walk of a bus at 11 PM (13% within a 10-minute walk), and about 1% of the district's warehouse and transport jobs are within a 5-minute walk of a bus at 11 PM (4% within 10 minutes). The same method gives 55% and 60% of residents near a bus at 11 PM in Cleveland and Miami. The app lets anyone check a trip and writes a request for better service. The link is [URL]. Could you tell me whether later or weekend service is being considered, and who I should send the numbers to? I am happy to share the method and data. Thank you, [name].
