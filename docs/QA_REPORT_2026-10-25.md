## Open (known limitations, not fixed)
- A place name can match a faraway namesake that is still within 70 km of the region centre (for example a typed "ZIP 95811" matched a car wash in Fairfield). The app then says no bus stop is within walking distance, which is true but unhelpful.
- Spanish has not been reviewed by a native speaker. The model section of About is English only in Spanish mode.
- The result tiles wrap to an orphan tile at 375 px when there are three tiles.
- Many chips and buttons are 36 px tall (above the 24 px WCAG 2.2 minimum, below the 44 px common guidance).
- No live delays or detours: the plan uses the published timetable. The Omnitrans alert feed needs a private token.
- 217 vs 218: HANDOFF.md says 217 deaths inside CA-33; the tagged file has 218 without the county filter. Cosmetic; the app's health card uses 217.
- Sacramento has no road-risk layers, fare file or employer list; Cleveland and Miami have no fare file.
- The offline behaviour was checked only through the 83 pwa checks, not by going offline in a browser.
